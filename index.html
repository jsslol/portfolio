    What I built

    Everything in the build is mine: design, code and tuning. The parts I'd point a lead programmer at:

    • A ScriptableObject content pipeline. Weapons, items and enemies are data assets, so adding a new weapon is a new asset, not new code.
    • Orbiting melee weapons that stack. Each knife, fork or spoon picked up joins the ring around the player and deals damage on contact.
    • A ten-wave director with a countdown timer, telegraphed spawn rings and scaling enemy mixes.
    • Progression: XP orbs, level-ups, currency and health, all surfaced in a minimal HUD.

    Challenges solved

    • Content at jam speed. With seven days, I couldn't hand-wire every weapon. Defining stats and behaviour in ScriptableObjects let me tune and add content from the Inspector in minutes.
    • Readable chaos. Late waves put dozens of enemies, projectiles and damage numbers on screen. Spawn telegraphs and color-coded pickups keep the player able to read the fight.

    Performance

    • Object pooling for enemies, projectiles, XP orbs and damage numbers, so late waves don't stall on instantiation and garbage collection.
    • Simple distance checks instead of physics queries for orbiting-weapon hits.
